# Joinery 0.5.0

Not released yet. Changes since [0.4.0](0.4.0.md).

## Share your game

- **Share**, a new tab beside Timeline, builds the game for the web, Windows,
  macOS and Linux with stock Godot 4.7 (`joinery share build`). Joinery keeps
  an export preset per target, zips each build with a README and licence
  notes, checks sizes against itch.io's limits, starts the build for your own
  computer once and lets the playtester play it. Export templates are pinned
  in `policy/export_templates.toml` and downloaded only when you press
  **Download and build**.
- **Store media** (`joinery share media`): 4 to 8 screenshots of different
  moments from a seeded run, a 30-second trailer recorded with Godot's Movie
  Maker and encoded in the offline worker, and a 630 × 500 cover composed in
  Krita. All from the game itself, with recipes in `share/recipes/`.
- **Page text** (`joinery share page`): an itch.io page drafted from the brief,
  the controls and the credits, saved to `share/page.md` for you to edit.
- **Publish to itch.io** (`joinery share itch`): pushes the builds with butler,
  itch.io's upload tool, using your own login. Only you can publish; agents
  are refused. butler is installed only with your consent, pinned in
  `policy/butler.toml` (ADR 0046).

## Make everything match

- **`/match`**, or **Make it match** in Settings, on the Review tab's art
  bible row and in the art direction, checks the whole project against the
  approved art bible and fixes what drifts (ADR 0045). The **Match** card
  shows one headline, a score per area (**Palette 72% · Models 100% · Sound
  90%**) and the most serious findings with what was measured and what the
  bible asks. `joinery match --project <path>` prints the same audit
  (`--json`, `--area`, `--plan`); no model is needed.
- It measures palette distance (CIEDE2000), texture sizes and texel density,
  model scale, pivots and budgets, material roughness and metallic, sound
  loudness and true peak, and two new optional tables in the bible's checked
  rules: `[environment]` (tonemap, exposure, fog, glow and colour grading of
  every Environment) and `[ui]` (allowed fonts, theme colours on the palette).
- **Fix all** (or **Fix** on one area) makes the fixes in the app that owns
  each file, as small reviewed changes running side by side: a palette-map
  layer or a resize in Krita (`edit_image`), apply scale, pivot, decimate or
  texel density in Blender (`apply_scale`, `set_pivot`, `reduce_triangles`,
  `set_texel_density`), material values, loudness on the rendered sound, and
  the Environment's or theme's values through Godot. Each change is checked,
  read by the reviewer and decided by you; after an accept the scores rise on
  the card.
- `[match] exclude` and `[match] final` in `joinery.toml` (or `joinery match
  --final <file>`) keep files exactly as they are.

## Fixed

- A Blender edit of a model whose scale wasn't applied failed its own checks:
  the copy of the old document the recipe keeps was judged as an asset.
  Nothing under `recipes/` is judged any more.
- Two parallel changes that each made a source folder clashed on its empty
  `.gdignore`; those markers are no longer claimed.
