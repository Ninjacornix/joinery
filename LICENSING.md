# Licensing

Joinery uses two licences, so that the tool stays open while the games you make
with it stay yours.

| Part | Licence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Joinery itself: `joinery/`, `servers/`, `bridge/`, `sandbox/`, `release/`, `policy/`, `docs/`, tests and everything else not listed below | [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE) | Improvements to Joinery, including ones offered as a hosted service, are shared back. |
| Files copied into your game project: the editor add-on (`godot_addon/joinery/`), `blueprints/`, `features/`, `templates/`, `kits/` and the sample `testbed/` | MIT (a `LICENSE` file in each folder) | Your game can be sold, kept closed source or released under any licence you choose. |
| Changes to the Godot engine: `godot_patches/` | MIT, like Godot itself | They can be offered upstream to Godot. |

The games, art and audio you make with Joinery belong to you. Joinery's licence
does not apply to them, and neither does the AGPL apply to a game because it was
made with Joinery or contains the MIT-licensed parts above.

Godot and other bundled components keep their own licences; see
[licenses/](licenses/). The creative tools (Blender, Krita, Surge XT and others)
are separate programs under their own licences.

Releases up to and including `v0.1.0-alpha.1` were published under the MIT
licence and remain available under it.
